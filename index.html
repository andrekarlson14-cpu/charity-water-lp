The 100% Model

Every dollar you give goes straight to the field.

Worried your money won't end up where it's supposed to? Private donors cover charity: water's operating costs, so 100% of public donations fund clean water projects. Not 90%. Not 95%. All of it.

And when the project is finished, we send you the proof: photos and GPS coordinates of the exact well, pump or filter you helped build.
